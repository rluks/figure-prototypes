Recommended routing — informational states only

These are the states where nothing is required of the cashier. The proposal: the CFD peek is the persistent ambient home — a quiet edge tab at rest, a small floating card while the customer is acting, expanding to a full live mirror on demand. The two discrete result moments (a customer got matched / signed up) surface in the persistent status chip above the operator name — it stays put, unlike a toast you'd glance away from. The error (CFD disconnected) earns a persistent strip, which stacks under the existing orange POS priority bar rather than fighting it.

Routing is a proposal, not settled product. Dropped from this slice: activity feed (more history than a cashier needs mid-transaction), blocking popover (reserved for the action states — confirm sign-up, pick profile, interrupt-to-charge — which live in a separate file), customer-row inline (no room inside the item list), and toast (nothing persistent — glance away and it's gone). Open question in #proj-cfd-loyalty: is a “customer didn't finish” signal worth a dedicated CFD→POS message at all?

The five treatments, compared

Treatment Footprint Best for Watch out for
CFD peek ★ On-demand · edge tab → card → full A single, always-there home for “what's on the CFD” that never blocks. Borrowed from Gmail's side panel — a labeled tab at rest, a compact card while active, expanding to a full live mirror. The panel overlays the tiles (no reflow). The tab's dot shows connection health (green/orange/red), separate from the activity color, and its placement is switchable (right · top-center · near-charge). New pattern for Register; the tab must be discoverable, and its best resting spot is still an open question (hence the placement switcher).
Status line Glanceable · text only A plain text line above the operator name — a screen icon (= CFD), the status text, and a state-colored dot. No chrome, so it tucks into the header without shoving content. Strong home for the two result moments (matched / signed up). One short line only; sits away from the catalog where taps happen.
Mirror card Rich · draggable · minimizes Empathy — the cashier sees roughly what the customer sees, so “help them” moments are obvious. Floats free: drag it anywhere, or minimize to a small bubble that docks out of the way. Biggest footprint when open; can pull the eye off ringing up. Relies on the cashier parking it sensibly.
CFD strip Ambient · tiny Always-on connection + steady state in one line. Reuses the real status-bar pattern; cheap to ship. Stacks directly under the orange POS priority bar so both can show at once (toggle it in the controls). One line, no history, easy to miss a change. Adds a second full-width band above the app.
Combined strip Ambient · one band for both Folds POS state and CFD state into a single band — POS on the left, CFD on the right, each with its own dot. Only one full-width strip instead of two stacked; the POS side tracks the priority-bar toggle. Crowded when both sides have something to say; a high-priority POS alert and live CFD activity compete for the same line.
